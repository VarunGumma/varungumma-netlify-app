Hey👋! I am Varun, a first-year PhD student in the School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Nanyang Technological University, advised by Prof. Soujanya Poria. Previously, I was a Pre-Doctoral SCAI-Center Fellow at Microsoft Research India, where I was mentored by Dr. Kalika Bali and Dr. Sunayana Sitaram. Prior to that, I completed my M.Tech at IIT Madras, co-advised by Dr. Raj Dabre and Prof. Mitesh Khapra, and earlier earned my B.E. in Computer Science (with a Minor in Physics) from BITS Hyderabad.

For a full list of my publications you can have a look here. Please feel free to reach out to me over email if you have any questions about my research, have suggestions or just want to chat, especially about Ph.D./MS opportunities and the applications cycle. (Just released a blogpost on it!)
